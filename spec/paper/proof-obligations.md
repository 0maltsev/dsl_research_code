# Formal results and proof obligations

Theorem labels are used only for arguments completed in the paper. All other claims below are Proof Obligations. G1/G2 testing can find counterexamples but cannot discharge an obligation.

## Paper proofs completed

| ID | Result | Proof basis | Residual boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| TH-CERT | A checked finite size/count certificate is semantically sound. | Induction on assumption, closed arithmetic, substitution, order, addition, scale, and maximum certificate nodes. | Certificate generation may be incomplete/untrusted; acceptance uses the checker only. |
| TH-TERM | Every closed well-formed source entry terminates on ABI-valid bounded input. | Strong induction on declaration rank, structural induction on expressions, literal-suffix induction, and residual-count induction for fold/builder. | Does not imply a target execution terminates. |
| TH-DET | Source evaluation is deterministic modulo rooted fresh-object isomorphism. | Induction on evaluation, parameter-complete ordered sequences, literal suffixes, and fold/builder residual count; canonical source NaN makes primitives functional. | Relies on the normative source rules; unreachable store content is quotiented. |
| LEM-ALIAS | The capacity-footprint sum over a multiset of value roots overcounts, and therefore bounds, the byte size of their reachable local-object union. | Type-depth induction; repeated identifiers and shared descendants can only repeat nonnegative terms in the sum. | It is an alias-safe upper bound, not an exact footprint equation. |
| LEM-ROOT-CLOSE | Restricting a store to the reachability closure of the declared current/future roots preserves lookup and traversal from those roots. | Least-reachability closure and restriction. | Does not permit a later root naming an object that was absent from the declared root set. |
| TH-FIN | Capacity substitution evaluates every accepted resource expression to finite naturals. | Declaration-order induction plus finite syntax/capacities/sums/recurrences. | Establishes definedness, not resource-bound soundness. |

## Trust boundary

The finite certificate checker, primitive table, ABI validator, fixed Wasm semantics/validator, and host closure assumptions are trusted until independently formalised. The source-to-BIR and BIR-to-Wasm transformations remain trusted while their obligations are open. AOT backend, native compiler, OS, runtime integration, and hardware are outside the source-to-Wasm relation. Artifact validation and differential testing provide evidence only for frozen artifacts.
